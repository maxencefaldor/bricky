# The K6 telephone kiosk

- Designed by Sir Giles Gilbert Scott in 1935. Cast iron, painted red, square in plan.
- About 8 feet (2.4 m) tall and 3 feet (0.9 m) square.
- A shallow domed roof sits on the arched heads of the four sides.
- At the top of every side, a white panel reads TELEPHONE, with a crown in relief above it.
- Three sides are glazed, the door among them. Glazing bars divide each glazed side into eight
  narrow horizontal panes, with narrow margin lights down both edges. The back is solid, framed
  by the same moulding.

Sources: the National Heritage List for England, entry
