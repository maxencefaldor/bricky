# Notre-Dame de Paris

- 128 m long and 48 m wide. The two west towers are 69 m tall, the spire (flèche) over the crossing
  reaches 96 m, and the nave roof is 35 m high.
- West façade: three portals (the central one shows the Last Judgment), a row of 28 statues of
  kings, the west rose window, 9.6 m across, and two square towers.
- Transept: north and south arms, each with a rose window; the south rose is 12.9 m across.
- Flying buttresses ring the nave and the apse; at the apse they reach 15 m from the wall.
- Limestone, off-white since its cleaning in 1963. The roof is lead on an oak frame, and so is the
  spire.
- A fire on 15 April 2019 destroyed the roof and the spire. The new spire was installed on
  16 December 2023, and the cathedral reopened on 7 and 8 December 2024.
